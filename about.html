About Us

Seeds of Kindness was founded in 2024 by Johannesburg volunteers who saw hunger on our streets. We are a non-profit community initiative.

Our mission: To cook and distribute nutritious meals to people experiencing homelessness every weekend, with dignity and kindness.

Our values: Compassion, Community, Accountability
